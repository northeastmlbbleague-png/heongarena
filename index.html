Privacy Policy

1. Information Collection

Heong Arena collects user account information, gaming credentials (such as Mobile Legends: Bang Bang ID and IGN), and transaction records to facilitate tournaments and store purchases.

2. Data Security

We use Firebase security protocols to safeguard user data, login tokens, and wallet balances against unauthorized access.

3. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal details with third parties except for tournament coordination via secure messaging channels like WhatsApp.

Terms & Conditions

Accounts

Users must provide accurate game credentials. Multiple accounts per user may lead to permanent suspension.

Tournaments & Cheating

Any use of third-party tools, hacks, map hacks, or unsportsmanlike conduct will result in immediate disqualification and account ban without refund.

Heong Coins (HC)

HC balances are non-transferable and can only be used within the Heong Arena platform ecosystem.

Administrator Decisions

All administrative decisions regarding tournament results, rules enforcement, and disputes are final and binding.

Refund Policy

Refunds are provided only in HC (Heong Coins). No cash refunds.

Store Refunds

Store refunds may be considered only when the order fails and the user has not received the item. No store refund after the user receives the product.

Tournament Refunds

Tournament refunds must be requested before the match pool is created. No tournament refund after the match pool is created, if the team fails to appear on time, or after the match has been played.

All refund decisions are reviewed and finalized by administrators.
